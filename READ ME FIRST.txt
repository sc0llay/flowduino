Flowduino 1.0
=============

Visual block editor for Arduino sketches, for macOS.

Drag Flowduino.app to your Applications folder, then read "Opening it the
first time" below — macOS will refuse to open it otherwise.


Requirements
------------

  * macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later
  * Apple Silicon or Intel — the app is a universal binary


Opening it the first time
-------------------------

This app is not signed with a paid Apple Developer certificate, so macOS will
say it "cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software".
That message is about the absent certificate, not about anything found in the
app. To open it anyway:

  1. Double-click Flowduino.app. Dismiss the warning.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to the Security section.
  3. Next to "Flowduino was blocked", click "Open Anyway", then confirm.

You only have to do this once.

If that option does not appear, open Terminal and run this, then try again:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Flowduino.app


What works without anything else installed
------------------------------------------

Everything to do with building a sketch: place and wire blocks, edit values,
watch the generated C++ update live in the code panel, and save and open
.flowduino files. You can also import an existing .ino sketch and have it
converted into blocks.

Compiling and uploading to a board are the exceptions. They need the Arduino
CLI, which is deliberately NOT bundled here — the app will offer to walk you
through installing it the first time you press Compile or Upload. Until then
those two buttons are the only things that will not work.


Getting started
---------------

A new document opens with just setup() and loop(). Blocks live in the sidebar
on the left, grouped by category — click a category to open it, or type in the
search box to find a block by name. Drag a block onto the canvas, then wire the
chevrons on the top and bottom edges to set the order things happen in, and the
circles on the left and right edges to pass values around.

Double-click any block to open its settings.


Keeping a canvas readable
-------------------------

  * Route a wire that runs behind a block: double-click the wire to drop a
    point on it, then drag that point out of the way. Double-click a point to
    remove it, or right-click it for "Straighten Wire".

  * Tidy Layout (Option-Command-T) rebuilds the whole arrangement from the
    wiring: control flow down the page, nested bodies stepped to the right,
    and the expressions feeding a block to its left.

  * Align to Grid (Option-Command-G) leaves your arrangement alone and only
    squares it up — the selected blocks, or everything if nothing is selected.

Both are on the toolbar, in the Canvas menu, and on the canvas right-click
menu. Everything is undoable with Command-Z.
